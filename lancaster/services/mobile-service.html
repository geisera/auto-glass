    Mobile Auto Glass Service in Lancaster

    You should not have to take time off work or rearrange your day to get a cracked windshield fixed. Keystone Auto Glass comes to you. Our mobile technicians perform chip repairs, crack repairs, and full windshield replacements at your home, your workplace, or any other location that works for you across Lancaster and nearby communities. We bring the tools, the glass, and everything needed to complete the job on-site. You stay put and we handle the rest.

    Where We Can Service Your Vehicle

    Our mobile service is designed to work wherever your vehicle is parked. Common service locations include:

    • Your home: A driveway or any flat outdoor surface with enough clearance for our technician to work around the vehicle comfortably. A garage can work if it has adequate overhead space and ventilation.
    • Your workplace: A parking lot or designated space where the vehicle can remain stationary for the duration of the job. We work around your schedule so your vehicle is ready when your shift ends.
    • Fleet yards and commercial lots: We service fleets on-site, working through multiple vehicles in a single visit when scheduling allows. Ask about fleet service when you call.
    • Apartment complexes and public lots: Any legal parking space with reasonable access is generally suitable. Let us know the location when you book so we can confirm access in advance.

    If you are unsure whether your location works for mobile service, call us and describe the setup. We will give you a direct answer.

    How to Prepare for Your Mobile Appointment

    A little preparation before your technician arrives helps the job go smoothly and on time:

    • Park on a level surface. A flat, stable surface is required for safe removal and installation. Sloped driveways may not be suitable for full replacements.
    • Allow at least 10 feet of clearance on the side of the vehicle where the damaged glass is located. Your technician needs room to work and to stage equipment.
    • Remove items from the dashboard and rear deck. Anything sitting on or near the dashboard can be dislodged during replacement. Clear the area before the technician arrives.
    • Disable your alarm system or be present to manage it. Vibration during glass removal can trigger vehicle alarms.
    • Check the forecast. Mobile replacements require dry conditions and temperatures above approximately 40 degrees Fahrenheit for the adhesive to cure correctly. We monitor weather and will contact you proactively if conditions require rescheduling.
    • Make sure the vehicle is accessible. If you will not be present during the appointment, confirm that someone can provide access to the vehicle and be available by phone in case we have questions.

    Service Window and Job Duration

    We provide a scheduled arrival window for every mobile appointment and send a notification when your technician is on the way. Here is what to expect:

    • Chip and crack repairs typically take 30 minutes or less. Your vehicle is ready to drive immediately when the repair is complete.
    • Full windshield replacements typically take 60 to 90 minutes on-site. After installation, you will need to observe a safe drive-away period before moving the vehicle. We will give you the exact wait time before we leave.
    • Arrival windows are scheduled in advance. If your technician is running early or delayed, we will contact you so you are not waiting without information.
    • Weather delays: If conditions deteriorate before your appointment, we will call to reschedule at the earliest available time. We do not perform replacements in rain or temperatures that would compromise the adhesive bond.

    Is Mobile Service Available for Insurance Claims

    Yes. Mobile service is available for both cash-pay and insurance-claim jobs. If you are filing a claim through your comprehensive auto insurance policy, we handle the billing with your carrier the same way we do for in-shop service. There is no additional cost to you for mobile service in most cases. Have your insurance information ready when you call and we will confirm coverage and schedule your appointment in a single call.

    What Mobile Service Cannot Accommodate

    Mobile service is suitable for most standard windshield repairs and replacements, but there are situations where an in-shop appointment is the better option:

    • ADAS static calibration requires a controlled indoor environment with calibration targets and diagnostic equipment. If your vehicle needs static calibration after replacement, we will coordinate an in-shop calibration appointment as part of the service. See our ADAS Calibration page for details.
    • Severely damaged vehicles with compromised body structure around the glass opening may require in-shop assessment before glass can be safely installed.
    • Adverse weather conditions including rain, snow, and temperatures below 40 degrees Fahrenheit prevent safe mobile replacement. Chip repairs have more flexibility but are still weather-dependent.

    When you call to schedule, we will identify whether mobile service is the right fit for your vehicle and situation and let you know clearly before you commit to an appointment.
