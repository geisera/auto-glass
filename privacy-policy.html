This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how Keystone Auto Glass ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information obtained through the website located at keystoneautoglass.com (the "Site"). Please read this Policy carefully before submitting any personal information. By using the Site or submitting a request form, you agree to the practices described herein.

1. Who We Are — Important Disclosure

Keystone Auto Glass operates as a lead generation and referral service. We do not perform auto glass installation, repair, or any other automotive services directly. Our business consists of collecting consumer service requests and sharing that information with independent third-party auto glass service providers ("Service Providers") who may contact you regarding your request. Understanding this business model is essential to understanding how your data is used.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit a request form on the Site, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number(s)
  • Email address
  • Street address or ZIP code / service location
  • Vehicle information (year, make, model)
  • Description of glass damage or service needed
  • Insurance information, if provided
  • Any other information you voluntarily include in a form or message

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our third-party service providers (including analytics and advertising platforms) may automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited, time on page, and referring URL
  • Cookies, pixel tags, and similar tracking technologies (see Section 7)
  • Click-stream data and interaction data

Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party data partners, advertising networks, or affiliate sources, which we may combine with information collected directly from you.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Lead Distribution: To share your service request and contact information with one or more Service Providers who may be able to fulfill your request.
  • Communication: To contact you via phone, text (SMS/MMS), or email in connection with your service request, including follow-up, confirmation, and satisfaction inquiries.
  • Marketing: To send you promotional communications about auto glass services or related offers, subject to your consent and applicable opt-out rights.
  • Site Operation and Improvement: To operate, maintain, analyze, and improve the Site and our services.
  • Analytics and Advertising: To measure the effectiveness of our advertising, optimize lead quality, and target advertisements to relevant audiences on third-party platforms.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or lawful requests from government authorities.
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity.

4. How We Share Your Information

Sharing your information with third parties is central to our business model. By submitting a form on the Site, you authorize the following sharing:

Service Providers (Lead Recipients)

Your name, phone number, email address, vehicle information, service request details, and other information you submit may be shared with one or more independent auto glass Service Providers. These providers may contact you directly to offer quotes or schedule service. You may be contacted by multiple Service Providers in response to a single submission. Service Providers receive your information for their own independent use and are not agents of Keystone Auto Glass.

Note on "Sale" of Data: Sharing your personal information with Service Providers in exchange for compensation — or for our commercial benefit — may constitute a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq., "CCPA") and similar state privacy laws. We disclose this practice transparently so you can make an informed decision before submitting your information. See Section 9 for your rights.

Marketing and Analytics Partners

We may share data with third-party advertising networks, analytics providers (such as Google Analytics), and marketing platforms to measure site performance, retarget advertising, and optimize our lead generation operations.

Technology and Operations Vendors

We share data with vendors who help us operate the Site, including hosting providers, form processing tools, CRM platforms, and email delivery services. These vendors are contractually restricted from using your data for their own independent marketing purposes.

Legal and Compliance Disclosures

We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, court order, or government request; to protect the rights, property, or safety of Keystone Auto Glass, its users, or the public; or in connection with fraud investigation or prevention.

Business Transfers

If Keystone Auto Glass is involved in a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or similar transaction, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice of any such transfer and any material changes to this Policy.

5. Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) — Phone and Text Communications

By submitting a form on this Site, you provide prior express written consent — as defined under 47 C.F.R. § 64.1200 — for Keystone Auto Glass and participating Service Providers to contact you at the phone number you provide using automated dialing systems, prerecorded or artificial voice messages, and automated text messages (SMS/MMS), even if your number is registered on a federal, state, or local Do Not Call registry.

Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any text message. Consent to receive autodialed or prerecorded communications is not a condition of obtaining any service.

6. Email Communications — CAN-SPAM Compliance

By providing your email address, you consent to receiving service-related and marketing emails from Keystone Auto Glass and Service Providers. All marketing emails will include:

  • Clear identification of the sender
  • A valid physical postal address
  • A clear and functional opt-out mechanism

You may opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any such email, or by contacting us at the address in Section 12. Opt-out requests will be honored within 10 business days as required by the CAN-SPAM Act, 15 U.S.C. § 7701 et seq. Transactional or service-related emails may still be sent after opting out of marketing communications.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We and our third-party partners use cookies, pixel tags, web beacons, and similar technologies on the Site. These are used to:

  • Recognize returning visitors
  • Analyze site traffic and user behavior
  • Deliver and measure the effectiveness of advertising
  • Enable remarketing to past visitors on third-party platforms (such as Google and Meta advertising networks)

You may control cookie settings through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect Site functionality. We do not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, but we do honor opt-out mechanisms described in this Policy.

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Pennsylvania Residents

Pennsylvania does not currently have a comprehensive consumer data privacy statute equivalent to the CCPA. However, you retain rights under federal law and the Pennsylvania Wiretapping and Electronic Surveillance Control Act (18 Pa. C.S. § 5701 et seq.) and the Pennsylvania Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law (73 P.S. § 201-1 et seq.). We are committed to fair and transparent data practices regardless of statutory mandates.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell.
  • Delete personal information we hold about you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Opt Out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. To opt out, contact us at the address in Section 12 or use our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Non-Discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising any CCPA rights.

To submit a verifiable consumer request, contact us at the information in Section 12. We will respond within 45 days as required by the CCPA.

All Users

Regardless of location, you may:

  • Request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you.
  • Opt out of marketing communications as described in Sections 5 and 6.
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us; we will honor such requests to the extent not required to be retained by law or legitimate business necessity.
